## Context and objective

This digital-transformation project explores a mobile application for driving-licence holders in France. The idea starts from motorists' everyday needs and asks whether a dedicated digital service could provide practical assistance and improve driving comfort.

## Method

The work remains at the design stage: formulate the idea, analyze user needs, assess its relevance, and then create a UX/UI prototype. The team uses the school project to test the initial intuition against feasibility and organize the intended uses in an interactive presentation.

## Implementation

Target users and expectations are studied before structuring the mobile journey. Screens and interactions are represented as a prototype so the concept can be tested and presented. An animated demonstration accompanies the report and illustrates the intended behavior without presenting it as a production application.

## Results and deliverables

The project delivers a complete report, a UX/UI concept, a prototype and an animated presentation of the motorists' application. These deliverables document the need, service idea and proposed experience for licence holders.

## Lessons learned

The work shows that a digital idea should be validated through needs analysis before development. Here, the prototype clarifies the journey, aligns the team and supports feedback without claiming that a finished product was deployed.
