## Context and objective

The study analyzes the ethical dilemma created by a treated-effluent leak from Prony Resources' South Plant in New Caledonia, detected on March 14, 2023. The marine outfall was damaged about one kilometer from the port at a depth of 50 meters, in an environment near reserves, a RAMSAR area and a UNESCO-listed site.

## Method

The report first presents the company and its environmental commitments, then compares those statements with the incident. The analysis examines operator responsibility, possible consequences for the marine environment and human health, a previous leak at the same location, regulatory compliance and the quality of information provided to stakeholders.

## Implementation

Available facts are assessed against prevention, transparency and responsibility principles. The proposed response combines accepting responsibility, an independent root-cause assessment, repair and restoration, stronger infrastructure and monitoring, rapid-alert protocols, and collaboration with local communities, authorities and environmental organizations.

## Results and deliverables

The engineering-ethics report formalizes the dilemma and a response plan. It recommends publishing complete incident information, assessing short- and long-term impacts, preventing recurrence and involving stakeholders in environmental monitoring.

## Lessons learned

The study distinguishes communicating CSR commitments from demonstrating responsibility during an incident. It emphasizes that prevention, independent expertise, transparency and community participation are necessary to restore trust in an environmentally sensitive industrial context.
