## Context and objective

The project addresses organizational transformation by considering human, organizational and technical dimensions together. Its objective is to build a path from initial diagnosis to deployment without reducing change to the rollout of a tool.

## Method

The approach combines a maturity assessment, stakeholder mapping, and communication and training plans. It identifies resistance likely to arise in the field and includes follow-up indicators and improvement loops.

## Implementation

Stakeholders are positioned according to their role and level of support. Communication actions are tailored to each audience, while training prepares operational adoption. Deployment is treated as a monitored progression: observe reactions, address resistance and adjust actions from feedback.

## Results and deliverables

The final report formalizes a complete transformation plan covering the starting position, stakeholders, communication, training, resistance management and monitoring. It provides a practical framework for steering adoption beyond the initial transformation decision.

## Lessons learned

Successful change depends as much on adoption and learning as on the technical solution. The project emphasizes identifying stakeholders early, distinguishing communication from training, and measuring adoption so support actions can be adjusted.
