## Begin with data
The CISA 2022 catalog identifies trends and actively exploited vulnerabilities. Qlik makes those signals readable.

## Observe exposure
Nmap characterizes hosts, ports and versions; Scapy reaches packet level. These views complement public data.

## Add prevention and protection
Phishing classification addresses the human factor, while cryptography exercises cover passwords and confidentiality.
